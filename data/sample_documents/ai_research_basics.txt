Title: Introduction to AI Research

Artificial intelligence research explores methods that enable
computers to perform tasks that typically require human intelligence.

Language models can summarize documents, answer questions, and
extract important information from text.

Document analysis systems should support their answers with evidence
from the supplied documents.

If the available documents do not contain enough information to
answer a question, the system should clearly communicate this limitation.

Retrieval systems identify relevant passages before an AI model
generates an answer based on those passages.
